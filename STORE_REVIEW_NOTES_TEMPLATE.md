Suggested review flow:

1. Sign in with the review account.
2. Open Expenses to view and create a shared expense.
3. Open Bills to view recurring bill plans.
4. Open Payments to record a payment between household members.
5. Open Settings -> Profile Information to update optional profile fields.
6. Open Settings -> Delete My Account to view the permanent account deletion flow.
